# Workflow: Think Out Loud

**Output: nothing.** No note, no file, no next action, no summary at the end.

That is the whole point and it is not a placeholder for a better output. Every other workflow in this vault produces an artifact, so every conversation bends toward one, and a half-formed idea cannot survive contact with a file format. It gets tidied into a heading and stops being the thing that was interesting about it.

**Triggers:** think out loud, think with me, bounce something off you, I am not sure yet, rough idea, no notes for this one.

---

## How to run it

**Short turns.** Three or four sentences. If you write six paragraphs the user stops thinking and starts reading, and one of you doing the thinking defeats the exercise.

**One idea per turn.** Not a survey of the space. Say the single most interesting thing you have and let them push on it.

**Say the unfinished version.** "I think X, and I have not checked" is the register. Waiting until a thought is defensible means only defensible thoughts get said, and those are rarely the useful ones.

**Disagree out loud.** Agreement adds nothing here. If their idea has a hole, say where.

**Do not summarize back.** Restating what they just said as a tidy list is the single most common way this fails. It feels helpful and it kills the thread.

**Do not resolve it.** Not every thread needs a conclusion. Ending on an open question is a legitimate result.

---

## How it ends

One of two ways, and the user decides which:

- **Nothing.** The most common outcome and completely fine. Something was thought about and not written down. Do not offer to capture it anyway.
- **They say to keep something.** Then, and only then, ask what specifically: usually one decision or one paragraph, not a transcript. Write it with [[System/Skills/Workflows/Capture|Capture]] or into an existing effort note.

Never write anything mid-session, and never end with "shall I write this up?" The offer itself pulls the conversation back toward producing something, which is what this workflow exists to avoid.

---

## When not to use this

- **A decision is genuinely needed.** Use [[System/Skills/Workflows/Decide|Decide]].
- **An idea needs attacking rather than exploring.** Use [[System/Skills/Workflows/Roast|Roast]].
- **The thing already has a shape and needs building.** Use [[System/Skills/Workflows/Start New Effort|Start New Effort]].

If a session starts here and turns out to need one of those, say so and switch. Drifting into it silently is how the exercise gets lost.

## Why this exists

Added 2026-08-05, after the vault's owner observed that agents here report rather than propose, and that half-formed ideas are most of what collaboration is. The rule count in [[Maps & Manuals/Me|Me]] supported it: 34 substantive rules, 11 prohibitions, 1 permission. The Working as a collaborator section was the first fix. This is the second, and the hypothesis behind it is that the deeper cause was structural rather than tonal: with every workflow producing an artifact, there was nowhere for an unfinished thought to live.

Untested. If sessions using it still end in tidy summaries, the cause was tone after all and this note should be deleted rather than expanded.
