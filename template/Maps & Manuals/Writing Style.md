# Writing Style

> **This file is empty on purpose, and that is safer than filling it in.**
>
> An earlier version shipped carrying the voice of the person who built the system:
> their turns of phrase, their second language, their family context, their surname
> in the title. Agents route every outgoing message through this file, so a borrowed
> voice does not sit harmlessly in the background. It gets used, and the result
> sounds like a stranger wearing your name.
>
> **Until this is filled in, agents write in plain, direct language and do not claim
> to imitate anyone.** That is a better default than confident imitation of the
> wrong person.
>
> Delete this block once the sections below hold real answers.

---

## How to fill this in

Two ways, and the second is much better than the first.

**From real writing.** Once email is connected, read thirty or forty messages this
person actually sent, across different audiences: a client, a colleague, a friend,
someone they were annoyed with. Fill the sections from what is on the page, not from
what they say about themselves. People describe their own writing inaccurately, and
almost always more formally than they write.

**By asking.** Slower and less accurate, but it works with no accounts connected.
Ask for the last three messages they were pleased with, and the last one they
rewrote four times. The gap between those is most of this file.

Either way: **quote real sentences.** A rule like "warm but direct" is unusable. A
line they actually wrote is not.
