## Conventions

- Add a row here whenever a person comes up in a way that context would be useful later.
- Create an individual note only when: career history, personal background, or agent-relevant context exists and is stable enough to write down.
- YAML for individual person notes: `type: person`, `relationship:`, `domain:`.
- Do not create person notes for people mentioned once in passing.
